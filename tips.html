Healthy Tips

A few small, practical habits worth trying — no fad diets, no gimmicks.

One simple habit worth building is drinking a glass of water before each meal. It helps you notice when you're actually hungry versus eating out of habit, and it naturally keeps portions a little smaller.

Try to make at least half your plate vegetables or fruit at lunch and dinner. It's an easy rule to remember, and it shifts your attention to what's actually on the plate.

Skipping breakfast tends to lead to snacking on whatever's around by mid-morning, so even something small like oatmeal or fruit makes a real difference.
