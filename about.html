About This Site

ABOUT THE PROJECT

🍴 Daily Meal Logger

A simple meal-tracking website focused on making everyday food logging easier.

WHY I BUILT IT

  • Simple meal tracking
  • Better support for everyday foods
  • Especially interested in indian dishes
  • Built as a class project
  • Continuing to grow

I built Daily Meal Logger because I wanted a simple way to track what I actually eat, instead of guessing at the end of the day. Most habit-tracking advice tells you to log your meals, but I never found an easy way to do that without an app pushing a subscription or a specific diet plan on me.

The bigger motivation is that most meal-tracking apps just don't handle Indian food well. Things like dal, roti, and idli either aren't in the database at all, or the portion sizes and calorie estimates are way off. I got tired of fighting with an app just to log a normal meal, so I decided to build something myself, shaped around the food I actually eat.

This started as a small class project, and I'm still learning HTML and CSS as I build it out. The plan is to keep expanding the meal library, especially with Indian dishes, and to eventually open it up so anyone can use it to track their own meals.
